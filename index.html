03 — How It Works

A clear path
from call to close-out.

Five steps. No mystery. You know what's happening, when it's happening, and what it costs — before the first ladder goes up.

  1. 01

    Contact

    Call, text, or request an estimate online. A real person responds — usually same day. We talk through what you're seeing and book a time that works.

  2. 02

    Inspect

    On-site walk of the roof. Photos, measurements, documented conditions. If there's a leak, we trace it. If the roof's fine, we tell you it's fine.

  3. 03

    Recommend

    Clear written proposal. Scope, materials, timeline, price — all of it on one page. No hidden line items. No upsell theater.

  4. 04

    Execute

    The work gets done correctly the first time. Materials matched to your roof. Job site stays clean. You get progress updates as we go.

  5. 05

    Close Out

    Final walk-through. Documentation, warranty, before-and-after photos. Roof gets handed back to you better than we found it.
